Pitcher projections blend Huronalytics’ own pitch-level model: a validated Stuff+ signal (season-blocked, two held-out seasons) adjusts each pitcher’s baseline by up to about half a win. Every MLB player and graded prospect carries two numbers: intrinsic surplus (projected on-field value minus remaining cost, in 2026 dollars) and market value (intrinsic adjusted by multipliers fitted on 2017–2026 trades: deadline rentals move at a premium, traded prospects and stars have historically returned less than intrinsic). Build both sides and the verdict compares market values against the spread of real trades.
